(actually, way more than enough about me)

  • my real (and unpretentious) name is James Vernon Cooper
    • i am known variously as James, Jim, Jimmy, 'Coop', and hopefully, Grandpa Jimmy (i've been lobbying hard for this one - sounds old, but also young to my ears)
  • i was born on July 14th 1960 in Long Beach CA (yes, i am as old as dirt, but always remember that dirt is very important and should be nurtured and held in high esteem)
  • i currently reside in Phoenix AZ, the valley of the sun

warning: if you venture any further down this page, you're going to be scratching your head and wondering, who does this guy think he is!

  • so... what is required to have an adventure such as mine?
    • a solid launching pad
      • my immediate family
        • i need look no further than my immediate family to realize that i am neither the best nor the brightest
        • my mother, father, and two brothers continue to serve as my most important and original inspiration
        • if i have any bragging rights at all, it's that i'm the best guitar player in my immediate family
          • but sometimes i wonder if, when they're together and i'm not around, they grab their guitars and play with such thunderous virtuosity that if i heard them, i would be dumbfounded and completely crushed
      • my extended family
        • whenever i'm with my nieces, nephews, aunts, uncles, cousins, in-laws, or their families, i'm overjoyed and delighted to be in their great company
        • my maternal grandfather Jasper played the fiddle with his brother Frank on guitar
        • my maternal uncle 'Red' played the guitar professionally
